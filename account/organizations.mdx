An **Organization** is the top-level container in Superlines. It holds your subscription, team members, API keys, and platform-wide configuration.

## Organization settings

Navigate to **Settings** in the sidebar to manage:

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Organization name** | Display name used in reports, invitations, and billing |
| **Website** | Your organization's primary website URL |
| **Subscription plan** | Current plan (Starter, Pro, Growth, Enterprise, or an Agency plan) |

## Subscription & usage

Your plan determines what's available across the entire organization. These are the current plans, matching the [pricing page](https://superlines.io/pricing):

| Resource | Starter | Pro | Growth | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tracked prompts** | 50 | 150 | 300 | 400+ custom |
| **Prompt frequency** | Daily | Daily | Daily | Daily |
| **AI engines** | Any 3, from all engines | Any 3, from all engines | Any 3, from all engines | Up to 10 |
| **Brands** | 1 | 3 | 7 | Unlimited |
| **Competitors** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Countries & languages** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Team seats** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **History in the platform** | 1 month | 2 months | 6 months | All time |
| **Data exports (CSV, Excel, JSON)** | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **API access** | — | — | Yes | Yes |
| **MCP server** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Action Center** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Article Generator** | 5 / month | 15 / month | 25 / month | Unlimited |
| **Schema Optimizer** | 10 / month | 25 / month | 25 / month | Unlimited |
| **AI Search Checker** | 10 / month | 25 / month | 50 / month | Unlimited |
| **Site crawl & audit** | — | Monthly | Weekly | Weekly |
| **Cross-Brand Analytics** | — | — | — | Yes |
| **SSO / SAML** | — | — | — | Yes |
| **Support** | Email | Email | Priority email | Slack / Teams, dedicated |

**Agency plans** (Agency Starter, Agency Pro, Agency Growth, Agency Enterprise) include everything in the same-named plan above, with a higher brand limit for client brands: 5, 10, 20, and unlimited respectively.

<Note>
Organizations that subscribed before the current plans were introduced keep the limits of their original agreement. Your **Subscription** page always shows the limits that apply to your organization.
</Note>

Usage is **aggregated** across all brands. For example, if Brand A uses 100 prompts and Brand B uses 50, your organization total is 150 prompts.

<Tip>
Monitor your usage on the **Subscription** page under Settings. If you're approaching limits, consider pausing unused prompts or upgrading your plan.
</Tip>

## LLM engine configuration

Choose which AI platforms to monitor. Your plan determines how many you can enable simultaneously: Starter, Pro, and Growth can enable any **3** engines from the full list below, and Enterprise can enable up to **10**.

### Available engines

| Engine | Type |
|---|---|
| **ChatGPT** | Online + Offline |
| **Copilot** | Online |
| **Perplexity** | Online |
| **Google AI Overviews** | Online |
| **Google AI Mode** | Online |
| **Gemini** | Offline |
| **Grok** | Online |
| **Claude** | Offline |
| **Mistral** | Offline |
| **DeepSeek** | Offline |

<Info>
**Online engines** can access current web information and tend to cite sources. **Offline engines** rely on training data and reflect historical brand authority.
</Info>

### Engine selection tips

- **Start with ChatGPT** — highest user base
- **Add Copilot** — covers Microsoft ecosystem
- **Add Perplexity** — strong for research-based queries
- **Add AI Overviews** — critical for Google search integration
- **Need more than 3?** — Enterprise can enable up to 10 engines

## API keys

API keys provide programmatic access to your Superlines data. They are available on **Growth and Enterprise** (and Agency Growth and Agency Enterprise). On Starter and Pro, connect to the [MCP server](/mcp/setup) with OAuth instead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Settings → Subscription or Brand Settings to find your API Key section" />
  <Step title="Click Generate API Key">
    A new key is created with the format `sl_live_...`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy and store the key securely">
    Keys are shown only once at creation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use keys for the [REST API](/api/authentication), [MCP server](/mcp/setup), and [Looker Studio](/integrations/looker-studio).

<Warning>
Never share API keys publicly. Store keys securely using environment variables. Rotate periodically and revoke compromised keys immediately.
</Warning>
