Superlines is an **AI Search Intelligence** platform built primarily for enterprise marketing teams and marketing agencies. It measures how brands appear across AI Search — mentions, position, share of voice, citations, sources and competitive performance — and gives teams the underlying data to see where visibility is gained or lost.

As AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews shape how people discover brands, Superlines gives marketing organizations one source of truth across brands, markets, languages and AI engines.

## What does Superlines do?

| Capability | What it means |
|---|---|
| **AI Search Monitoring** | Track how often your brand is mentioned in responses from ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Claude, Grok, Mistral, and DeepSeek. |
| **Competitor Benchmarking** | See how your Share of Voice compares to competitors across every LLM. |
| **Citation Tracking** | Know when and where your website is cited as a source in AI responses. |
| **Content Opportunities** | Discover topics and queries where you can improve your AI visibility. |
| **Prompt Management** | Track the questions your audience asks AI — and measure your performance on each one. |
| **Team Collaboration** | Role-based access so your whole team can monitor and act. |

## Who Superlines is for

Superlines is built for two primary audiences:

- **Enterprise marketing teams** — measure and improve AI Search visibility across brands, products and markets. Brand, SEO, content, communications, digital, growth and analytics teams share one source of truth for how AI platforms represent the company, how competitors perform and which sources influence answers.
- **Marketing agencies** — manage AI Search visibility across client portfolios from one platform. Benchmark competitors, identify opportunities and report progress across brands, markets and AI engines.

Superlines is particularly strong for organizations that manage multiple markets, brands, languages and AI engines, large prompt sets, many competitors and centralized reporting across a complex marketing organization.

Self-service plans are available for smaller teams that need the same intelligence at lower data volumes. Those plans do not change who the platform is built for.

Use Superlines where your team already works. Connect through [MCP](/mcp/overview) to tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and n8n, or use the [API](/api/authentication) to bring granular AI Search data into your own dashboards, applications and reporting workflows.

See [Use Cases](/getting-started/use-cases) for how these teams work in the platform.
